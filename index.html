Metabolic / Obesity

Retatrutide

A novel triple agonist targeting GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors for significant body weight reduction and glycemic control.

  • Initial Dose: 5mg - 10mg
  • Protocol: Subcutaneous (Weekly)
Regenerative

BPC-157

Cytoprotection and structural repair compound. Accelerates tendon, ligament, and muscle repair mechanisms.

  • Dose: 2mg - 5mg
  • Protocol: 5x Weekly (1 Month)
Anti-Aging

GHK-Cu

Copper tripeptide that increases collagen and elastin expression, improving skin density and global firmness.

  • Dose: 2mg - 5mg
  • Protocol: Subcutaneous (5x Weekly)
Recovery

TB-500

Promotes neovascularization in ischemic zones and modulates inflammation for structural remodeling.

  • Stack: BPC-157 + GHK-Cu
  • Focus: Musculoskeletal Repair
Anti-Inflammatory

KPV

Tripeptide used for systemic inflammation reduction, decreasing pro-inflammatory cytokines like IL-1β and TNF-α.

  • Stack: Tissue Regeneration
  • Focus: Cytokine Modulation
Wellness / Energy

NAD+

Crucial coenzyme for mitochondrial function, DNA repair, and overall cellular energy restoration.

  • Focus: ATP Synthesis
  • Benefits: Cognitive & Cellular Longevity
Growth / Metabolic

GH Secretagogues

Including Ipamorelin, CJC-1295, and GHRP-6. Used for body recomposition, recovery acceleration, and hormonal balance.

Longevity / Sleep

Epitalon

A synthetic pineal peptide that stimulates telomerase activity and melatonin production, regulating circadian rhythms and cellular aging.

  • Focus: Telomere Elongation
  • Benefits: Deep Sleep & Rejuvenation
